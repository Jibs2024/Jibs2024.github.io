Residential Construction

Whether you're building from the ground up, renovating, extending, or adding to your property, Stud and Beam brings the same hands-on trade knowledge to every residential project, regardless of scale.

Not every project starts with a blank block. Most residential work is renovations, extensions, granny flats, and outdoor additions, projects that need to work around an existing home, existing services, and a family still living there. That takes a different kind of care than a new build on an empty site, and it's where our hands-on experience matters most.

From structural framing through to final finishes, our team manages the full construction process directly, working with your architect, engineer, and certifier to keep the project moving and the communication clear, whatever the scope.

We work across NSW on custom homes, renovations and extensions, granny flats and secondary dwellings, pools, and outdoor living structures, along with dual occupancy and design-and-construct arrangements where clients want a single point of accountability.
