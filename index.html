About the Company

Our Story

A building company founded on the belief that the best outcomes come from people who genuinely understand construction.

Stud and Beam Constructions was built from the ground up. Founded by Jibril El-Ahmad, a qualified carpenter and builder with years of hands-on experience, the company was born out of a belief that better outcomes come from people who genuinely understand construction at every level.

The premise is simple: when you understand the work from the inside, you make better decisions at every stage of a project. You spot problems before they become defects. You communicate more honestly with clients, engineers, and subcontractors. You take pride in the finished product because you know exactly what went into it.

Today, Stud and Beam operates across three core streams: new builds, remedial works, and commercial fit-outs, delivering projects for private clients, developers, strata managers, and institutional clients across Sydney, Newcastle, and Wollongong.

We're not trying to be the biggest contractor in the market. We're focused on being the most reliable, for the clients we work with.

What we stand for
Honesty before the job starts
We tell you what the project actually involves, including the parts that are inconvenient. No scope creep surprises.
Compliance as a baseline, not a ceiling
We build to Australian Standards because it's the right way, not because someone is checking.
Respect for the site and the people on it
Safe, tidy, and considerate, whether it's a strata building with residents or a live commercial tenancy.
Accountability after handover
We don't disappear once the job is done. If something needs attention, we come back.

New Builds

Custom residential homes and structural builds delivered with care, precision, and end-to-end project management.

Building a home is one of the most significant projects a person undertakes. Stud and Beam approaches every new build with the understanding that the details matter, because the people running it have been the ones installing them.

From structural framing through to final finishes, our team manages the full construction process with a level of involvement that larger contractors simply can't offer. We work directly with your architect, engineer, and certifier, keeping the project moving and the communication clear.

We work across Sydney, Newcastle, and Wollongong on custom homes, dual occupancy projects, additions and alterations, and design-and-construct arrangements where clients want a single point of accountability.

Scope of works
  • Custom residential new homes

  • Dual occupancy & secondary dwellings
  • Home additions & upper floor extensions
  • Design & construct (D&C) delivery

Service — 02

Remedial Works

Specialist remediation for buildings that need more than a cosmetic fix — ACP cladding, waterproofing, concrete, and heritage conservation.

Remedial work requires a builder who understands what went wrong, not just what needs to be replaced. Stud and Beam's remedial capability is grounded in genuine construction knowledge, which means we diagnose accurately and fix properly.

We work with strata managers, building owners, developers, and institutional clients across Sydney, Newcastle, and Wollongong to address everything from ACP cladding non-compliance to waterproofing failures, concrete spalling, and heritage conservation works.

Our team has delivered remedial projects for institutional property managers, strata schemes, and private building owners across NSW: clients who need a contractor that can operate within occupied buildings, coordinate with engineers and consultants, and deliver outcomes that hold up under scrutiny.

Scope of works
  • ACP (aluminium composite panel) cladding replacement
  • Waterproofing remediation — balconies, podiums, roofs
  • Concrete cancer repair & spalling treatment
  • Structural crack repair & façade restoration
  • Heritage & conservation works
  • Sealant replacement & joint treatment
  • Post-tension slab repair
  • Strata & body corporate remediation programs
Who we work with
  • Strata managers & owners corporations
  • Building owners & developers
  • Engineers & building consultants

Commercial Fit-Outs

Retail, office, and hospitality fit-outs delivered on programme, within budget, and built to a standard your clients will notice.

Commercial fit-out projects have tight timelines, live neighbours, and clients whose business depends on the outcome. Stud and Beam understands the stakes, which is why we plan thoroughly, communicate proactively, and treat every tenancy as if it were our own.

We work with property managers, tenants, landlords, and head contractors across retail, office, and hospitality projects. From strip-out and make-good through to full fitout delivery, we manage the process end to end.

Our commercial work spans institutional property managers, retail operators, and direct-to-tenant fit-outs across Sydney, Newcastle, and Wollongong.

Scope of works
  • Retail store fit-outs & refurbishments
  • Office fit-outs & open plan reconfiguration
  • Hospitality & café fit-outs
  • Tenancy strip-out & make-good
  • Partitioning, joinery & cabinetry
  • Suspended ceilings & raised access flooring

  • Base building upgrades
